Shikoku Akiya Database

Vacant houses & land from municipal akiya banks in Kochi and Ehime, Shikoku, Japan — translated and summarised in English.

How to buy: Akiya-bank properties are handled by the municipality, not by this site. Most cities require you to register with their akiya bank (often with an interview) before viewing or negotiating. Foreign nationals can buy real estate in Japan without a special visa, but you will need a Japanese address for registration after purchase, a tax agent if you live abroad, and cash — rural akiya rarely qualify for mortgages. Renovation, registration taxes and agent fees are extra; budget them separately.

Data: LIFULL HOME'S Akiya Bank public listings, collected . Prices change and listings close without notice — always confirm on the source page. USD figures are approximate at ¥155 = $1. This site is an independent English index, not affiliated with LIFULL or any municipality.
